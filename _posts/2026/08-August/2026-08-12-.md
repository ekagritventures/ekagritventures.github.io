#### Economics of AI

[Some Simple Economics of Open versus Closed AI](https://www.a16z.news/p/some-simple-economics-of-open-versus)

Interesting read. A few themes have been emerging in AI reads across are:

- Intelligence will be metered.
- Open Models will win for enterprise applications, because at some point the marginal improvement is not the thing that matters. The reliability and the ease-of-doing the work matters.
- AI labs cannot pursue every path of innovation. Open models helps diffusion as well as the pursuit of these paths.
- Complementary assets(prop data, distribution, infra, tacit knowledge) will lead to moats.
